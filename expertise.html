Applied AI

The same discipline, with new tools.

AI is useful when it fits the business. I combine language models with rules, search and evaluation.

How an agent decides

This is how Sentinel Engine handles a request. Select each step.

The customer writes in their own words, in Spanish or Portuguese.

The model only identifies what the customer wants. It doesn't decide anything.

The bank's policy, data protection and validations live in code, not in the prompt.

Nothing runs until the customer confirms. When needed, the case goes to an advisor.

Every decision is recorded, and every version passes an evaluation before release.

  • RAG and semantic search

    Search by meaning, not just keywords, with answers that cite their sources. Knowman uses Postgres with pgvector and local models with Ollama.

  • Evaluation and testing

    Every prompt and every change is measured against reference data before release, and the results are saved so they can be compared.
